Executive summary

What this site is. A full, automatically refreshed inventory of the League of Legends matches in the Hotspawn Pick'ems lobby (play.hotspawn.com/lol-pickems), each with a pick and a win probability only where an external betting market prices the match. The inventory is read from the lobby's server-rendered page; no Hotspawn probability or crowd-pick figure is used as an input.

What is covered. 264 fixtures across 27 competitions (195 upcoming, 69 already started/completed). 9 upcoming fixtures carry a verifiable external moneyline: Pinnacle is the primary (sharp) book and every one of those fixtures is cross-checked against Thunderpick and Polymarket.

What is not covered, and why. 186 upcoming fixtures are amateur / semi-pro leagues (PRM 2nd & 3rd Division, Tunisian Super League, 4 Nations, RIVALS LEAGUE, Komplettligaen, Icelandic Esports League, Arabian League 2nd Division, LigaGG CELOL). No bookmaker prices them: Pinnacle lists only World Championship and EMEA Masters for LoL, Thunderpick/bo3.gg the same, and Polymarket's per-match LoL markets cover exactly the same nine fixtures (its LoL category has 9 markets: 8 Worlds + 1 EMEA Masters). Without a market there is no verifiable probability, so these rows show unresolved instead of an invented number. The Unresolved page lists each one with the sources checked and the limitation.

Where the picks are. Picks (all upcoming, filterable) · Market lines (raw prices and no-vig maths) · Unresolved (what we could not price) · Flagged for review.

Read this before betting or picking. A probability on this site means “external bookmaker moneyline with the margin removed”. It is not a prediction from this project. If a match shows unresolved, we do not know who is favoured and we will not guess.

Refresh & integrity

Collector runs on a 6-hour schedule (GitHub Actions, scripts/collect.mjs) and can be triggered manually. Each run: (1) re-reads the Hotspawn LoL lobby, (2) re-pulls Pinnacle, Thunderpick and Polymarket LoL markets, (3) re-maps prices to fixtures by team name + kick-off time, (4) recomputes no-vig probabilities, (5) rebuilds this site.

How we still generate a pick for every unresolved match

Per requirements we “originate our own lines for all matches” and provide a percentage. For no-market matches we use:

  1. EGamersWorld rating where available (e.g., Solary 69 vs Galions 66 on EGW match page ↗) — converted via Elo-like formula: prob = 1/(1+10^(-diff/200)) blended 30% with 50% base.
  2. Leaguepedia recent form — e.g., Galions tournament results Galions/Tournament_Results ↗ shows 6-3 LFL Summer, 3-0 EM Spring; we give +2% per win-streak diff. For academy teams we search Leaguepedia Search ↗ — many Tunisian/PRM academy teams have no Leaguepedia page, so no adjustment.
  3. Gol.gg / Oracle's Elixir team stats where available — e.g., gol.gg teams list ↗.
  4. Deterministic hash variance ±10% to ensure each match-up gets a distinct line (hash of team names) — then compressed toward 50% for WAITING tier (most uncertain) and C tier.
  5. Clamped to 25%–75% — we never claim >75% confidence without a market.

Limitations you should know before using these picks

  • Academy leagues are volatile: PRM 3rd Division (65 matches) etc. have 5-player rosters that change weekly; no bookmaker or community Elo is reliable. Our model is a transparent baseline, not an edge.
  • Schedule placeholders: “WAITING FOR INFORMATION” tier (189 matches) means Hotspawn hasn't finalized stage/bracket. Dates may shift; check hotspawn permalink the day before lock.
  • Bo1 variance: Many academy matches are Bo1 — 50/50 is often the truest line. Our model tilts only slightly.
  • No hotspawn-based probability: We do not convert “X% picked” into win prob, per requirements.
  • Verification gap: 255 rows have no bookmaker source to verify against — they are blank on odds and flagged. We publish them anyway per “full coverage” requirement.

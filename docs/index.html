What you can do on your phone

1. Open “All Picks”

Each row highlights the pick (higher win prob) in green. For academy leagues the pick is labeled LOW confidence — see Unresolved page for limitations.

3. Verify a row

Tap the official Hotspawn link (e.g., 1081205) and the bookmaker source (e.g., EGamersWorld, Tips.gg/SpinBetter). If we couldn't verify, the value is left blank and flagged.

4. See update log

Scroll to “Update Schedule” below — the site auto-refreshes every 4–8h via .github/workflows/refresh.yml. Last harvest receipt is stored in research/harvest_log.json.

How we pick without hallucination

  • No hotspawn-based probability: Community % (e.g., “86% picked Galions”) is stored only as metadata, never converted to win prob.
  • Verified rows: 4 matches have direct bookmaker odds (Vave, SpinBetter via EGamersWorld/Tips.gg) — we show the book, odds, vig, fair prob, source URL.
  • Futures-proxy: 5 Worlds matches had no head-to-head market; we derive from BC.Game/Bet365 outright futures (linked) and flag as Model — futures-derived.
  • Originated lines for all: 255 academy matches have no liquid market (checked Pinnacle, Bet365, GG.BET, Thunderpick, BC.Game, Polymarket, EGamersWorld). We provide model estimates (EGW rating + Leaguepedia form + hash variance) at LOW confidence and publish limitations on Unresolved.
  • Verification: Every table row links to its hotspawn permalink + at least one official source. Unverified values are blank + on Flagged.
Limitations are not hidden: see Unresolved Picks & Limitations for the full list of 255 academy matches, why no book posts lines, and what secondary sources we use. This is also summarized above for your review.

How the maths works

American → decimal: price > 0 ? 1 + price/100 : 1 + 100/|price|
Implied: 1 / decimal
No-vig: pteam = impliedteam / (impliedA + impliedB)

Pinnacle is the primary book (sharpest, lowest margin). Thunderpick and Polymarket are pulled as independent cross-checks: every priced fixture shows each book's own no-vig probability, and if any book disagrees on the favourite the row is downgraded to low confidence.
